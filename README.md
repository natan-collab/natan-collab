# Natan Zaidenweber

Founder and CEO, [TrustDynamics](https://trustdynamics.ai) · San Francisco Bay Area

The AI build-out and the energy transition are constrained by a risk that still goes unpriced: whether the data centers, power plants and mines they depend on win the consent of the communities around them. We are building the engine to price that risk.

**What we measure.** From hearing records, permits, agreements, court filings and conflict registers, we map the network around a project: who is connected to whom, which unresolved community demand can reach a decision the project depends on, who can act on it, and whether the developer's commitments answer what was asked.

**Two cases so far**
- Prince William Digital Gateway, Virginia: a data-center rezoning voided in court on a notice defect that was public before the vote. [The risk was in the record](https://nzaidenweber.substack.com/p/the-data-center-industry-keeps-coming) · the article.
- Peru: sixteen years of the national ombudsman's conflict register, about 2,400 conflicts, as a network, with a multi-source record for the Cajamarca mining region.

**Before this.** Founded StakeWare, a stakeholder-management platform used by ConocoPhillips and Marathon Oil. Twenty-seven years on one question, since Shell's Camisea project in Peru: how commitments win, or lose, a community's consent.
